# Code of Conduct

Project Echo is committed to providing a welcoming and respectful environment for everyone who participates in the project.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to feedback.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's credentials, personal information, audio, transcripts, or private project material.
- Keep discussions relevant and professional.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sustained disruption, sexualised misconduct, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Maintainer response

Project maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behaviour violates this code. Reports should be sent privately to the maintainers through a channel listed on the repository profile. For security issues, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This code of conduct applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other community channels operated for Project Echo.
